* All SCN0 offsets are relative to start of SCN0 data: at the magic number
* Padding is not accounted for, Swift automatically does that

RRSCN File Format:
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
u32                 | 'RRSC'                | Magic Number
u32                 | compressionType


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
u32                 | 'SCN0'                | Magic Number
u32                 | versionNumber         | if SCN0 layout changes, this is required to still decode older versions
u32                 | fileSize              | Length in bytes of the whole file
u32                 | sectionCount          | how many sections there are, for example in the case of: (INFO, DLTA) = 2 sections; sectionCount = 2        


Table Of Contents:
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

---[ Entries (count: sectionCount) ]--- 
u32                 | fourCC                | Four character code for section, for example: 'VERT'
u32                 | flags                 | settings
u32                 | totalSize             | size of section payload (data excluding this header)
u32                 | dataOffset            | offset to payload (the data)



Sections:
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

---[ Uniforms (Magic Number: `UNI0`) ]---
uuid_t (8 bytes)    | sceneId
float3              | cameraPosition
simd_quatf          | cameraOrientation
float               | cameraMoveSpeed
float               | FOV
float               | zNear
float               | zFar


---[ Vertices (Magic Number: 'VERT') ]---
u32                 | vertexCount           | amount of vertices present in this section
(Repeats `vertexCount` times) (struct Vertex)
float3              | position
float3              | normal
float2              | UV


---[ MaterialDatas (Magic Number: 'MAT0') ]---
u32                 | materialCount
(Repeats `materialCount` times)
float4              | diffuseColor (Kd) (x, y, z), dissolve (w)
float4              | emissionColor (Ke) (x y z), emissionIntensity (w)
float4              | params: roughness (Pr) (x), metallic (PM) (y), IOR (z), isSky (w)
u16                 | diffuseTextureIndex (map_Kd)
u16                 | dissolveTextureIndex (map_d)


---[ Rasterizer Indices (Magic Number: 'INDC') ]---
u32                 | indexCount
(Repeats `indexCount` times)
u32                 | index


---[ RayTracer Faces (Magic Number: 'RIDC') ]---
u32                 | indexCount
(Repeats `indexCount` times)
u32                 | index


---[ RayTracer Triangle Material Map (Magic Number: 'RTMM') ]---
u32                 | triangleCount
(repeats `triangleCount` times)
u32                 | materialIndex


---[ SubMeshes (Magic Number: 'SUBM' ) ]---
u32                 | subMeshCount
(Repeats `subMeshCount` times)
u32                 | vertexOffset
u32                 | vertexCount
u32                 | indexOffset
u32                 | indexCount
u32                 | materialIndex
u32                 | meshIndex


---[ Meshes (Magic Number: 'MESH' ) ]---
u32                                 | meshCount
(Repeats `meshCount` times)
uuid_t (8 bytes)                    | uuid
uuid_t (8 bytes)                    | parent
u32                                 | nameOffset
u32                                 | subMeshOffset
u32                                 | subMeshCount
u32                                 | childrenCount
[uuid_t (8 bytes); childrenCount]   | children
float3                              | pivot
float3                              | position
float3                              | rotation
float3                              | scale
float3                              | localMinBounds
float3                              | localMaxBounds


---[ BLAS Nodes (Magic Number: 'BNOD') ]---
u32                 | blasNodeCount
(Repeats `blasNodeCount` times)
float4              | minBoundsX
float4              | minBoundsY
float4              | minBoundsZ
float4              | maxBoundsX
float4              | maxBoundsY
float4              | maxBoundsZ
int4                | childIndices
int4                | faceOffsets
int4                | faceCounts


---[ TLAS Instances (Magic Number: 'TINS) ]---
u32                 | tlasInstanceCount
(Repeats `tlasInstanceCount` times)
float4x4            | modelMatrix
float4x4            | invModelMatrix
float3x3            | invNormalMatrix
i32                 | blasStartIndex


---[ TLAS Nodes (Magic Number: 'TNOD') ]---
u64                 | tlasNodeCount
(Repeats `tlasNodeCount` times)
float4              | minBoundsX
float4              | minBoundsY
float4              | minBoundsZ
float4              | maxBoundsX
float4              | maxBoundsY
float4              | maxBoundsZ
int4                | childIndices
int4                | tlasInstanceIndices



---[ MaterialMetas (Magic Number: 'MATM') ]---
u32                 | materialMetaCount
(Repeats `materialMetaCount` times)
uuid_t (8 bytes)    | uuid
u32                 | materialNameOffset
u32                 | materialDataIndex
u32                 | diffuseTextureNameOffset (0 = no diffuse texture)
u32                 | dissolveTextureNameOffset (0 = no dissolve texture)



---[ String Pool (Magic Number: 'STRP' ) ]---
// The first string is always the Scene name
u32                 | stringCount
(Repeats `stringCount` times, Exists out of fat-arrays of characters):
u32                 | stringLength (including padding '\0' bytes for u32 alignment)
[u8; stringLength]  | characterArray
