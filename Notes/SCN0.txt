* All offsets are relative to 0, the start of the main SCN0 file, in other words, they are absolute positions, pointers


SCN0 File Format:
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
u32                 | 'SCN0'                | Magic Number
u32                 | versionNumber         | if SCN0 layout changes, this is required to still decode older versions
u64                 | fileSize              | Length in bytes of the whole file
u32                 | sectionCount          | how many sections there are, for example in the case of: (INFO, DLTA) = 2 sections; sectionCount = 2
u32                 | padding               | -
u64                 | tocOffset             | offset to Table Of Contents



Table Of Contents:
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

---[ Entries (count: sectionCount) ]--- 
u32                 | fourCC                | Four character code for section, for example: 'VERT'
u32                 | flags                 | settings
u64                 | totalSize             | size of section payload (data excluding its header)
u64                 | dataOffset            | offset to data



Sections:
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

---[ Vertices (Magic Number: 'VERT') ]---
u64                 | vertexCount           | amount of vertices present in this section

(Repeats `vertexCount` times) (struct Vertex)
float3              | position
float3              | normal
float               | padding
float2              | UV


---[ Materials (Magic Number: 'MAT0') ]---
u64                 | materialCount

(Repeats `materialCount` times)
float3              | diffuseColor (Kd)
float3              | emissionColor (Ke)
float               | dissolve (d)
float               | roughness (Pr)
float               | metallic (Pm)
u16                 | diffuseTextureIndex (map_Kd)
u16                 | dissolveTextureIndex (map_d)
u16                 | isSky
u16                 | padding


---[ Rasterizer Faces (Magic Number: 'FACE') ]---
u64                 | facesCount

(Repeats `faceCount` times)
uint3               | vertexIndices
i32                 | subMeshIndex


---[ Raytracer Faces (Magic Number: 'RFAC') ]---
u64                 | raytracerFacesCount

(Repeats `raytracerFacesCount` times) - TODO: missing padding
struct Vertex       | vertex0
struct Vertex       | vertex1
struct Vertex       | vertex2
i32                 | materialIndex


---[ BLAS Nodes (Magic Number: 'BNOD') ]---
u64                 | blasNodeCount

(Repeats `blasNodeCount` times)
float4              | minBoundsX
float4              | minBoundsY
float4              | minBoundsZ
float4              | maxBoundsX
float4              | maxBoundsY
float4              | maxBoundsZ
int4                | childIndices
int4                | faceOffsets
int4                | faceCounts


---[ TLAS Instances (Magic Number: 'TINS) ]---
u64                 | tlasInstanceCount

(Repeats `tlasInstanceCount` times)
float4x4            | modelMatrix
float4x4            | invModelMatrix
float3x3            | invNormalMatrix
i32                 | blasStartIndex
[u32; 3]            | padding


---[ TLAS Nodes (Magic Number: 'TNOD') ]---
u64                 | tlasNodeCount

(Repeats `tlasNodeCount` times)
float4              | minBoundsX
float4              | minBoundsY
float4              | minBoundsZ
float4              | maxBoundsX
float4              | maxBoundsY
float4              | maxBoundsZ
int4                | childIndices
int4                | tlasInstanceIndices




---[ TextureLinks (Magic Number: 'TEXL' ) ]---
u64                 | textureLinkCount

(Repeats `textureLinkCount` times)
u16                 | textureIndex          | To which index the texture maps to
u16                 | padding               | -
u32                 | textureNameOffset     | At this offset is a fat-array of characters that gives the filepath to the texture 


String Pool:
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Exists out of fat-arrays of characters:
u32                 | stringLength
[u8; stringLength]  | characterArray
